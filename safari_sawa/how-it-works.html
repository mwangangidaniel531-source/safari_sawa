How it works

From request to ride in three simple steps.

Choose your service

Select boda boda for a quick trip or matatu for a fixed route or group journey. Use the fare calculator to estimate your cost.

Send your booking

Enter your pick-up point, destination, number of passengers and preferred service. Send your request through WhatsApp and wait for confirmation.

Confirm and pay

Confirm the fare and trip details with the provider. Pay cash or M-Pesa as agreed, and request the official M-Pesa payment details when applicable.

Before your trip

  • Check that your pick-up point and destination are correct.
  • Confirm the final fare before departure.
  • For boda boda trips, wear a helmet.
  • For matatu trips, use the available seat belt.
